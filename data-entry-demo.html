Example: review and normalize entries

Goal: standardize dates, remove extra spaces, identify duplicates, and flag missing information rather than guessing.

Repeatable checklist

  1. Compare each entered value with its source.
  2. Apply a consistent date and identifier format.
  3. Check for missing fields and duplicate identifiers.
  4. Flag uncertainty for review rather than inventing values.
  5. Recheck the cleaned output before submitting.

Relevant skills: accuracy checks, structured data, record review, issue documentation, file organization.
