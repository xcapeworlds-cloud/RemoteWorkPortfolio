Written support practice.

Examples of how I would organize customer questions and draft clear responses.

Order status question

Issue: A customer asks where their order is.

Next step: Confirm the order number and check tracking details before providing a status.

Draft reply: Thanks for reaching out. I can help check your order status once the order number is confirmed.

Account access problem

Issue: A customer cannot sign in.

Next step: Review approved account recovery steps and escalate if necessary.

Draft reply: I'm sorry you're having trouble signing in. Let's go through the available account recovery steps.

These are fictional practice scenarios, not real customer tickets.
